Your money, your rules.

SuMal is a private, fast money manager for tracking income, expenses and savings. Your data is encrypted and lives only in your own Google Drive.

Application Features

Everything you need to stay on top of your money — without leaving your data behind.

🧾

Transactions

Income, expense and saving entries with an in-app calculator and expression input.

🏷️

Categories

Fully customisable — 139 icons across 16 groups plus 14 theme-aware colours.

🎯

Budgets

Monthly limits per category with live progress tracking.

💹

Savings

Term-based savings plans with precise interest calculation.

📊

Reports

Date-range summaries and CSV export for your own records.

🕐

History

Day-grouped timeline with search, filters and inline edit/delete.

Encryption
